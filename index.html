RISK ASSESSMENT

Public Risk Level: LOW — The WHO and CDC continue to assess the overall risk to the general public as low. No evidence of sustained community transmission has been identified as of May 13, 2026.

Transmission: The Andes strain is among the very few hantaviruses with documented person-to-person transmission in close-contact settings. Spread is not airborne over long distances. Standard contact/droplet precautions are in effect for confirmed cases.

Ongoing Concern: The WHO has warned nations to prepare for additional cases due to the virus's long incubation period (7–39 days) and the fact that approximately 40 potentially exposed individuals dispersed internationally before full contact tracing was initiated.
